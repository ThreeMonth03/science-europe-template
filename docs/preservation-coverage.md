# 0.3.7: restore preservation answers before inventing new questions

This experiment restores 16 previously unreferenced Common KM questions to Q11.
It does not use an LLM, change the KM, or declare Q11/Science Europe complete.

## What the audit established

The local DSW 4.30 server compiled the pinned public English and Traditional
Chinese Common KMs 2.7.0. Each has 592 reachable question IDs. That number is an
inventory size, not a coverage denominator or a claim that every question belongs
in a Science Europe DMP. `scripts/audit_question_destinations.py` records full
paths, option membership, and static references, without project answers.

The bounded before-state is in
[the bilingual binding contract](../requirements/preservation-bindings-2.7.0.json).
It binds the inventory and source templates to hashes, and records the actual
question paths and options for the 16 additions. The complete local inventory
also revealed further unmapped material; it is not all implemented this round.

| Information | Before 0.3.7 | This experiment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Produced-dataset description, processing stage, related paper | No source-template reference | Q11 dataset block; paper only under its selected stage |
| Non-publication reason and custom explanation | No source-template reference | Q11, attributed to the questionnaire; never a destruction decision |
| Post-project cold storage and its 10 follow-up questions | No source-template reference | One project-wide Q11 subsection, independent of dataset publication |
| Measured-data re-use and other-field explanation | Already in Q13 | Retained there; no name-based association with Q11 datasets |
| Initial selection of datasets/versions to retain or destroy | Not established by the reviewed bindings | Explicit review notice, not an invented policy |

The source guidance is Science Europe, *Practical Guide to the International
Alignment of Research Data Management*, Extended Edition (January 2021),
[researcher guidance and evaluation rubric](https://www.scienceeurope.org/media/4brkxxe5/se_rdm_practical_guide_extended_final.pdf),
DOI 10.5281/zenodo.4915861, CC BY 4.0. The Q11 contract distinguishes selection
and retention rationale, future re-use, deposit location, and repository
arrangements. Merely displaying a repository and duration is insufficient to
establish all these topics.

Two tempting substitutes have deliberately not been repurposed:

- The raw-data archiving choice `ba381732-f499-419f-8744-e13807af83e3` lives under
  workspace management. Its alternatives discuss existing copies or cheaper
  remeasurement. It is not a general per-dataset long-term selection policy.
- `eb698404-004a-4cdf-a599-be01e0eb7fd2` asks about selecting *additional* research
  outputs; its guidance explicitly distinguishes these from datasets. It could
  support a separately labelled future output section, not a fabricated answer
  about selecting Q11 datasets.

## Content and reading boundaries

- Dataset descriptions, custom non-publication reasons, and custom archival
  lifetime arrangements remain independent Markdown blocks, including multiple
  paragraphs/lists. They are not appended inside fixed sentences.
- Non-publication reasons include raw/intermediate data, unpublishable results,
  re-use value, cost, loss, and custom explanations. These are supplied claims,
  not the template's assessment. Non-publication does not imply non-preservation.
- Cold storage after the project is a **project-level** question. It is printed
  once, not copied under every dataset or treated as a publication repository.
  An explicit No is limited to this kind of storage; a blank is not turned into No.
- The Yes branch retains payer, minimum lifetime (including supplied years or
  a custom arrangement), extendability, limitations, extension authority and
  each selected renewal criterion, obsolete-format handling, and media migration.
  Missing follow-ups are visible without suppressing completed facts.
- Extension criteria are scoped to renewing the archive. They do not establish
  the initial dataset/version selection policy, hence the Q11 review notice.
- Stale publication child replies do not activate repository/catalogue text
  when the dataset's publication choice is No or unanswered.
- No CSS or Word filter changes are required. The existing owned-paragraph and
  free-answer boundaries are reused. Actual PDF/DOCX validation remains required.

## Tests and maintenance

Four deterministic bilingual scenarios are generated by
`scripts/generate_preservation_fixtures.py`: complete, partial, custom lifetime,
and explicitly no post-project cold storage. They include two datasets to catch
accidental duplication of project-level information. The fixture validator now
also checks MultiChoice option membership, not merely reachability.

`tests/test_preservation_coverage.py` checks scope, option branches, partial
answers, stale children, separate author paragraphs, all renewal-criterion
subsets, and the bilingual compiled binding contract. The legacy duration test
now checks its own retention warnings rather than falsely treating a correct
duration as proof that no other Q11 review is needed.

The Chinese repo's `probe_preservation_translation.py` exercises generated
English/Chinese branches and compares fixed statements with the reviewed phrase
list. It is an offline adapter probe, not native rendering or full acceptance.

The three small source partials, Q11, and UUID definitions are in the upstream
upgrade review surface. The official baseline stays at 1.30.1; partial adoption
is not an upstream version upgrade. English 0.3.7 and independently versioned
Chinese output are locked by commit. Both use `feat/preservation-coverage` for
this experiment, not a permanent branch for every wording change.

Remaining content work includes exact preservation selection/mapping, archive
location and sustainability detail, reference-data maintenance, recurring costs,
and explicit negative/missing paths in other existing Q11 branches. More output
is useful only when it preserves relevant answers and their actual scope.
